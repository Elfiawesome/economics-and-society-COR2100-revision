Exam mode

Two hours. Fifty questions.

The simulator builds a balanced paper from all 12 topics: 25 MCQs and 25 written questions. MCQs are marked automatically. Written answers are revealed with a model answer and a marking checklist, then you self-mark out of 2. Every question is worth 2 marks, so the full paper is out of 100.

Before you start

The timer starts immediately and keeps running. You can move between questions, flag them, and return. Answers are saved in this browser, so a reload will offer to resume.

Marking honesty

For written questions, do not award yourself full marks for a vague answer. The checklist is deliberately specific: formula, substitution, result, interpretation. Partial = 1, missed = 0.
